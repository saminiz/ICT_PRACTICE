# ICT_PRACTICE
Information and Communication Technology (ICT) is more than just tools and screens; it is a vital bridge that connects people, ideas, and opportunities across the world.
To me, ICT represents empowerment and limitless learning. It turns local experiences into global conversations by letting me access endless knowledge through the internet, stay close to loved ones via smartphones, and solve everyday problems using smart digital tools. It shapes how I work, create, and understand the world around me.
